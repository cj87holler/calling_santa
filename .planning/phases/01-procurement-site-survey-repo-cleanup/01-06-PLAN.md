<objective>
Put a hard, enforcing spend ceiling on the OpenAI account and revoke the orphaned browser-destined
key — before any unattended runtime exists to spend against it.

Purpose: OPS-03 is not hygiene. The Realtime API bills per audio token against a 12-hour-a-day,
seven-day schedule with unsupervised children as the load generator, and the research season model
puts plausible monthly spend at $180 to $1,080 — potentially exceeding the entire hardware budget.
The existing $5 prepaid balance is a real ceiling today but an accidental one that disappears on the
first top-up and fails closed mid-call, with Santa vanishing while a child is talking to him (D-19).

This plan also discharges the revocation obligation recorded in plan 02. D-23 fixes the order as
check, then rotate, then delete: plan 02 checked, this plan rotates, plan 09 deletes. Deleting first
would orphan a live credential with no record it ever existed. Because plan 09's deletion is
irreversible and destroys the last on-disk record of that credential, the record this plan writes is
a **structured token**, not prose — a heading that always exists cannot prove a revocation that might
not have happened.

Output: four dashboard screenshots and `SPEND-CEILING.md` carrying the configured figures and the
revocation token.
</objective>
