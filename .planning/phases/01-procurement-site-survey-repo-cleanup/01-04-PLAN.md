| Boundary | Description |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-1-13 | Physical safety / Denial of Service | Laptop lithium battery in a sealed outdoor enclosure | mitigate | Battery removal is a non-negotiable `PRE-INSTALL OBLIGATIONS` checklist item on the `laptop` branch (D-12), asserted by an acceptance criterion rather than left to prose |
| T-1-14 | Tampering | Counterfeit or marked-up Pi board from a marketplace seller | mitigate | Pricing and ordering are restricted to `pishop.us` and `canakit.com`, both official Raspberry Pi resellers; an acceptance criterion asserts Amazon marketplace pricing was not used as a source |
| T-1-15 | Denial of Service | Undersized USB-C supply causing USB current-limit throttling | mitigate | The 27W 5V/5A official PSU is a mandatory ORDER LINE on any board-only Pi selection, with the December failure mode named so it is not silently dropped as an optional extra |
| T-1-16 | Denial of Service | Single point of failure on the USB audio adapter or microSD card | mitigate | Two audio adapters from different vendors or chipsets and two microSD cards are mandatory order lines; the cost is roughly $22 against a season-ending single-point failure in late November |
