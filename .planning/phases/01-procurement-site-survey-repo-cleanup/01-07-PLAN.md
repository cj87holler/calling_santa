<objective>
Measure the install spot, choose the connectivity path on those numbers, and order what the chosen
path needs.

Purpose: HW-06 is the only requirement in this phase whose evidence an agent physically cannot
produce. D-09 makes it explicit that a decision without a cited number does not satisfy it. Research
pitfall 3 names the failure precisely: someone reads -58 dBm on a Sunday morning, writes "WiFi", and
the installation drops calls every December evening when the street's networks are loaded, the leaves
are gone and the rain is not.

**This plan is branch-aware.** Read `evidence/site-survey/CONNECTIVITY-BRANCH` first — it was written
by plan 03 and contains exactly `ethernet` or `wireless`. Under `ethernet`, the wireless survey
reduces to a feasibility record and the measurement is of the wired path (D-07). Under `wireless`,
the full RESEARCH.md protocol runs (D-08). Do not run both, and do not run the full survey under the
ethernet branch just to be thorough — that is a weekend spent on a number nobody will use.

Output: raw measurement files, `CONNECTIVITY-DECISION.md` citing them, and `connectivity-order.md`.
</objective>

  <action>
Analyse the raw output, write the decision, and record the resulting order.

Compute from every `wifi-samples-*.csv`, per window and across all windows: min, median and p95 RSSI
in dBm, and min and median tx bitrate. **Report p95 and min, never the mean** — the p5 RSSI and the
p95 jitter are what break calls, and an average hides both. From the `mtr-*.json`, extract the final
hop's loss percentage, standard deviation as a jitter proxy, and average RTT. From the
`iperf3-*.txt`, extract sustained throughput in each direction and the count of zero-throughput
seconds in the per-second series.

Write `evidence/site-survey/CONNECTIVITY-DECISION.md` with these sections.

`## Measurements` — a table of every computed statistic against its threshold, marked pass, marginal
or fail. The pass bar: RSSI at or above -67 dBm; SNR at or above 25 dB; packet loss below 0.5%;
jitter below 30 ms; RTT to `api.openai.com` below 100 ms; sustained throughput at or above 2 Mbps in
each direction. Prefix each measured value with the literal token `MEASURED` so the numbers are
distinguishable from the thresholds at a glance. Include the derived bandwidth requirement that
justifies the 2 Mbps bar: Realtime audio is PCM16 at 24 kHz, roughly 384 kbps raw each way, roughly
512 kbps after base64 framing over WebSocket, so the real requirement is about 1 Mbps in the worst
case and 2 Mbps is the number to test against so an evening-loaded shared connection still has room.

`## Decision` — a line reading `DECISION: Ethernet`, `DECISION: WiFi` or `DECISION: LTE`, followed by
the specific measured numbers that justify it. D-09 makes this mandatory: a decision without a cited
number does not satisfy HW-06. Under the ethernet branch the decision must contain the literal word
`Ethernet` and a `Mbps` figure; under the wireless branch it must contain `WiFi` or `LTE` and a `dBm`
figure.

If the wireless branch measurements land in the marginal band, say so rather than rounding up to a
pass. A marginal result triggers an LTE evaluation: the dongle must be 4G/LTE — never 3G, those
networks are shut down by the major North American and European carriers — and the data plan must be
at least 20 GB per month against the derived call volume of roughly 3.4 MB per minute, roughly 400 MB
per day, roughly 12 GB per month at 30 calls a day of 4 minutes. That rules out small IoT data plans
outright. Note that the LTE quality bands (RSRP at or above -90 dBm, RSRQ at or above -10 dB, SINR at
or above 15 dB) are conventional industry values flagged ASSUMED in research, adequate for a go or
no-go but not sourced.

`## Caveats` — the proxy-device caveat if the survey device was not the production Pi, with the
Phase 3 re-survey obligation restated; the WAN-variance caveat if the iperf3 host was not wired; and
the seasonal caveat that August leaf cover is a best case for a December path.

Then create `evidence/orders/connectivity-order.md` as an order record with the same mandatory field
set the other order files use — Item, Vendor, SKU, Quantity, Actual price paid, `Order #`, Order
date, Delivery ETA, Spec qualifier confirmed. Populate the item lines from the decision:

Under `DECISION: Ethernet` — outdoor-rated or direct-burial Cat6 at the `Length to order (ft)` figure
from `ETHERNET-SCOPING.md`, **never indoor PVC-jacket cable, because UV and moisture destroy it
within a season**; a PoE injector or PoE switch plus a PoE splitter matching the compute path chosen
in plan 04; and a note that the weatherproof extension cord is **removed** from the BOM because one
PoE cable now carries power and network. The IP68 gland is already ordered by plan 05.

Under `DECISION: WiFi` — an outdoor extension cord with **SJTW or SJEOW jacket, 12/3 or 14/3,
wet-location "W" rated**, at the length measured in `ETHERNET-SCOPING.md`. A 16/3 light-duty holiday
cord is fine for LED strings and marginal for a continuous-duty appliance.

Under `DECISION: LTE` — the SJTW or SJEOW cord as above, plus a 4G/LTE USB dongle verified against
the intended carrier's band support (Huawei MS2372h-class dongles are documented working with
ModemManager on Raspberry Pi), plus a data plan of at least 20 GB per month with a hard data cap.

Leave the price and order-number fields blank if the purchase has not been made yet, and record in
the plan summary that `connectivity-order.md` awaits a purchase.

Do not commit a street address, a full postal address, GPS coordinates, an SSID, or a WiFi password.

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Physical measurement -> committed evidence | Every later phase trusts these numbers; a single reading presented as a survey is undetectable downstream |
| Home network internals -> public repository | Scan dumps, SSIDs and address-identifying detail would be committed permanently |
| Survey device radio -> production device radio | A proxy laptop's antenna is not the Pi's antenna; the difference is worth 5 to 10 dB |
| Outdoor cable jacket -> one December of UV and moisture | Indoor PVC-jacket Cat6 fails within a season and the failure is not visible until it is |
